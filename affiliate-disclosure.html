Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: February 15, 2026. In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines, this page explains how our earnings model operates and why we use affiliate referral links on Smart Deal Hub.

What is an Affiliate Link?

An affiliate link is a specific URL containing a unique tracking code assigned to us. When you click on one of these links on our site and purchase a digital product, the merchant platform (such as Gumroad, WarriorPlus, ClickBank, or Lemon Squeezy) registers that purchase as a referral from Smart Deal Hub.

As a thank you for referring the buyer, the product creator pays us a referral fee or a percentage of the sales price as a commission. This transaction is entirely handled by the merchant platform's secure automated tracking systems.

How Does This Affect Your Cost?

This referral process has absolutely no effect on the price you pay. You will pay the exact same amount (and sometimes even less, if we negotiated a special discount code for our readers) whether you use our affiliate link or go directly to the vendor's page yourself.

Our Commitment to Editorial Integrity

We take our reviews and recommendations very seriously. Our business model relies on the ongoing trust of our readers. If we write deceptive, overly hyped reviews to generate sales, users will experience buyers' remorse, demand refunds, and never trust our hub again.

Here is how we maintain strict independence and editorial integrity:

  • Cons & Limitations are Mandatory: Every product review on our site lists specific limitations, cons, and criticisms. If a tool has a bad user interface, lacks advanced exports, or has a high recurring cost, we say it directly.
  • We Do Not Accept Paid Positive Placements: No software developer or video course author can pay us to receive a favorable rating or five stars. All of our reviews are based on our hands-on evaluation criteria.
  • Transparent Testing: On our About page, we detail our actual review process, including direct account testing, Aggregated Forum reviews, and price-to-value math.

Why Do We Use Affiliate Marketing?

Running a comprehensive, clean, and high-performance digital review site requires resources. Commissions generated from affiliate links help support our costs, which include:

  • Paying for server hosting, domain registrations, and security certificates
  • Purchasing accounts and subscription tiers for software tools to test them properly
  • Hiring experienced technology writers and video specialists to conduct deep analysis

We deeply appreciate when readers choose to buy through our affiliate links, as it allows us to keep the site entirely free of pop-ups, obstructive ads, and paid user accounts.
